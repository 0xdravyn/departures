CALENDARS

The Today view shows your next event under the date, time and weather. To connect a calendar:

  1. Open Google Calendar on the web, then Settings, and pick the calendar on the left.
  2. Scroll to "Integrate calendar" and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  3. Paste it below, hit Test, then Apply to TV.

The address is stored on the TV only, never shown again and never shared anywhere else.
