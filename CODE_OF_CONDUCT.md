# Code of conduct

Departures is a small project kept by one person in their spare time. A few house rules keep it pleasant:

- Be kind and assume good faith. People arrive with different TVs, different skills and different first languages.
- Keep issues about the board, not about each other. Disagree with an idea as much as you like; do not go after the person.
- No harassment, no slurs, no personal data about anyone (including calendar contents and screenshots that show them).
- The maintainer decides what gets merged, and may close or lock a thread that stops being useful.

Something crossed a line? Email the maintainer through the address on the GitHub profile, or open an issue titled "Conduct" without the details, and it will be taken seriously and kept private.
